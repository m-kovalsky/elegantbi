Here is a [COVID-19 report](https://bit.ly/2RRVHIK) I made and published via [Power BI Publish to Web](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/service-publish-to-web). All COVID-19 case data are based on Johns Hopkins University's published [dataset](https://github.com/cssegisanddata/covid-19/tree/master/csse_covid_19_data/csse_covid_19_daily_reports). I have also integrated COVID testing data provided by [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/covid-testing) and [The COVID Tracking Project](https://covidtracking.com/api).
